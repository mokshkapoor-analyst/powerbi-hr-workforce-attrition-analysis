# 📁 Dataset Overview

This project analyzes an **HR workforce dataset** containing **1,470 employee records**.

The dataset includes:

- Employee demographics  
- Department & job roles  
- Monthly salary  
- Salary hike percentage  
- Work-life balance  
- Job satisfaction  
- Environment satisfaction  
- Overtime status  
- Distance from home  
- Years at company  
- Attrition status  

The dashboard helps organizations understand workforce structure, salary patterns, and factors driving employee attrition.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem & Analysis

Organizations often struggle to identify why employees leave and which workforce factors contribute most to attrition.

The main objective of this project is to:

- Analyze employee attrition trends  
- Understand workforce distribution  
- Evaluate salary competitiveness  
- Identify attrition drivers  
- Improve workforce retention strategies  

The dashboard converts HR operational data into actionable insights for better workforce management and decision-making.

# 📊 Key Insights & Findings

| Insight | Finding |
|---|---|
| Overall Attrition Rate | ~16% |
| Highest Attrition Department | Research & Development |
| High-Risk Salary Segment | Employees earning below $5K |
| Most Affected Age Group | 26–35 years |
| Overtime Employees | 34% |
| Work-Life Balance Impact | Poor balance increases attrition significantly |
| Distance Impact | Employees living 21–25 km away show highest attrition |
| Marital Status Trend | Single employees show highest turnover |
